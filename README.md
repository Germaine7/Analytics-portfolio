# Data Analytics Portfolio — Jermaine

A selection of data analysis projects focused on turning data into decisions:
customer and product segmentation, dimensionality reduction, and exploratory analysis
with business recommendations.

## Projects

### [AllLife Bank — Credit Card Customer Segmentation](./customer-segmentation)
Segments a bank's credit-card customers into three actionable groups using K-Means,
Gaussian Mixture Models, and K-Medoids, validated with silhouette scores. Each segment
maps to a specific marketing or service strategy.
*Python · scikit-learn · PCA · clustering*

### [Vintage Vehicle Segmentation with PCA & t-SNE](./vehicle-segmentation-pca-tsne)
Uses PCA and t-SNE to reduce correlated vehicle attributes to their underlying structure
and identify two market segments for a used-car dealership's merchandising strategy.
*Python · scikit-learn · PCA · t-SNE · K-Means*
